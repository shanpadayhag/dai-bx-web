Cards can now move by themselves. Give one a schedule and it lands at the top of the column you chose, on a date or every time a routine comes round.

## Card schedules

- A card's details have a **Schedule** section with up to 20 schedules. Each one is **Once** (move to a column on a date and time) or **Repeating**.
- A run moves the card to the top of its column within two minutes of the time. If a run is missed, only the latest one catches up, marked late. Runs missed while a schedule is paused never catch up.
- A repeating schedule can **block calendar time**: each run adds a planned event named after the card, with the length and category you pick.
- **Pause**, **Resume**, **Run now**, **Edit** and **Delete** from each schedule's menu. **Run now** moves the card straight away and keeps the next run as it was.
- **Runs** lists each run, newest first, with late and by-hand marks and the time tracked on the card.
- Deleting a schedule's column pauses it and asks you to pick another. Deleting the card deletes its schedules.
- Scheduled cards show a clock on the board, with the next move on hover or focus. The calendar shows each upcoming move as a thin flag; pick one to open its card.

## Repeating events

- Planned events repeat with the same picker as schedules: daily, weekly on the day, monthly, yearly, every weekday, or **Custom** (every N days, weeks, months or years, on chosen weekdays, a day of the month or its nth or last weekday, ending never, on a date or after N times).
- Moving, resizing, editing or deleting one occurrence asks whether it's for **This event**, **This and following events** or **All events**, and changes only what you pick.

## Time zones

- **Settings → Time zone** sets the calendar's zone. It follows your device until you pick one, and the calendar, its days and its flags redraw in that zone.
- New events and schedules start in the calendar's zone. Each one can have its own under **More options**.
- A repeat keeps its local time in its own zone, so daylight saving doesn't move it.
