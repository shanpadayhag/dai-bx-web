**Today** is now the one screen for right now. It reads your real timers, calendar and tasks, and it keeps a record of your day as you go.

## Running now

- Every running task or card gets its own card, with its timer, when it started, its estimate and the next check-in.
- **Finish**, **Check in** and **Open in calendar** sit on each card.
- When nothing is running, Today says so and points you to Up next.

## On the calendar

- Today's planned and tracked events in order, with their category color. Events that already ended are muted.
- A planned event linked to a task has a **Start** button, or a check once the task is done.
- **Day view** opens the calendar on today.

## Up next

- Tasks with a block planned for today come first, in block order. A block whose start has passed is marked **Missed** until you start the task.
- Then the tasks from groups due now, ordered the way the planner orders them: due date, then priority, then their place in the group.
- Running tasks are left out. Each row shows its due date, priority and estimate, with **Start** and a tick.
- Five rows show at first. **Show all** lists the rest.
- The list updates on its own when a group's schedule changes, a planned block starts, or the day ends.

## Activity

- Starting, finishing and checking in, ticking and reopening tasks, moving, archiving and restoring cards, schedule runs and accepted plans each add one line to your activity.
- A finish says how long you tracked, whether you marked it done, and when it really ended.
- Today shows today's activity. **See all** opens the **Activity** page, grouped by day, with **Load more** for older entries.
- An entry keeps its names after the task or card is deleted.

## Also

- The made-up count next to **Today** in the sidebar is gone.
- Today follows your calendar time zone and moves to the next day at midnight, even in a background tab.
