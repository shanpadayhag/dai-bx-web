DaiBX started as a nested to-do list. Version 3 rebuilds it from scratch as one workspace for **tasks, a board and a calendar**, so the same piece of work shows up in all three without being copied.

This release brings accounts and tasks. The board and the calendar are designed and show sample data; they come to life in the next releases.

## Accounts

- Sign up with an invite, confirm your email, and protect the account with an authenticator app and recovery codes.
- Reset a forgotten password by email.
- See where you're signed in and sign out of any session, or all of them.
- Change your name, email, password, theme and authenticator, get new recovery codes, or delete the account and everything in it.

## Tasks

- **Groups** hold tasks nested as deep as the job needs, up to 32 levels.
- Add, rename, estimate and delete at any level. Completing a task completes everything under it; reopening it reopens the tasks above it.
- Reorder by dragging or from a task's menu, and move a task with all its subtasks into another group.
- **Import a group** from a JSON file. It always arrives as a new group, beside any group with the same name.
- Every change shows at once and rolls back with a message if the server refuses it.

## Coming next

- **Board:** lists and cards, with your own properties on each card.
- **Calendar:** starting a task opens a calendar event and finishing it sets the end, with check-ins for when you step away.
- **Workflows:** cards that move themselves when their tasks start or finish, on a date, or on a routine.

## Under the hood

- The frontend moves from SolidJS to **Next.js**.
- The API is **Rust and Axum** on AWS Lambda, with **Postgres** on Supabase as the one store.
- DaiBX 2 is retired; its code stays on the `v2` branch.
