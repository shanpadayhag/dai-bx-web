A group can now keep its own rhythm. It shows up when it's due and starts fresh on the days you choose, so a routine can stay in one group instead of being imported again.

## When a group shows

- **Group settings** have a **Shows** row, **Always** by default. Pick a repeat to limit the group to those days.
- **Between** narrows it to part of the day. Leave both times empty for all day. An end before the start runs past midnight and belongs to the day it started.
- The Tasks page has a **Due now / All groups** toggle, and this device remembers your choice.
- In **All groups**, a group that isn't due shows when it's next due, like "Next: Mon 06:00", or **Ended** once its repeat has finished.
- A group you have open stays open when it stops being due, marked **Not due now**.
- Finishing a group's tasks doesn't hide it. It stays in **Due now** until its day or window ends.

## When a group unchecks

- An **Unchecks** row, **Never** by default, sets its own repeat and an **At** time, 00:00 unless you change it.
- From that time, every task in the group shows unticked again, even if the app was closed.
- Group times follow your calendar's time zone.
- The page updates by itself when a group's window starts or ends or an uncheck passes, and when you come back to the tab.

## Card schedule blocks

- **Block calendar time** now works on one-time schedules too.
- An optional **Starts at** gives the block its own time: the card moves at 08:00 and its block runs 10:00–11:00. Leave it empty to start at the run time.

## Repeating events

- Choosing **Does not repeat** on a repeating event now works. **This and following** ends the series and keeps this event on its own. **All events** leaves just this one event.
- Changing an event's repeat asks only **This and following events** or **All events**, because a new repeat can't apply to one event alone.

## Fixes

- Tapping an input on an iPhone or iPad no longer zooms the page.
