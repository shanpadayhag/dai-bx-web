**Notifications** bring DaiBX to you. A check-in no longer waits for you to open the app, and a schedule run or a planned block tells you when it happens.

## Turning them on

- **Settings → Notifications** has a **This device** row. **Turn on** asks your browser once, and **Send a test** checks it works.
- Turn them on separately on each phone, tablet and computer you use, up to ten devices.
- On iPhone and iPad, add DaiBX to your Home Screen first. Settings shows the steps.
- The bell in the top bar shows whether this device gets notifications and turns them on in one tap.

## What arrives

- **Check-ins:** when a running task or card reaches its check-in. Tap it, or **Check in**, to keep tracking, or **Finish** to end it now. It follows your check-in setting in Time tracking.
- **Schedule runs:** when a card schedule moves a card. Tap it to open the card, or **Start card** to start tracking it.
- **Blocks starting:** when a planned block on your calendar starts, with **Start** for a linked task.
- Each switches on or off on its own. Each one arrives once per device, and anything more than five minutes late is dropped rather than sent.

## Quiet outside working hours

- With **Quiet outside working hours** on, nothing arrives outside your working hours.
- A check-in for work you started outside your working hours still arrives, so a late session doesn’t run on unnoticed.

## Devices and privacy

- **Devices** lists every device that gets notifications, when it was added and when one last reached it. **Remove** stops them on that device.
- Signing out, or ending a device’s session, stops notifications on it.
- Notifications travel through your browser’s push service (Apple, Google, Mozilla or Microsoft), encrypted so it can’t read them.
