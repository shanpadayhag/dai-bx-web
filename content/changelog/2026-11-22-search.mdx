**Search** finds your work from anywhere. Press **⌘K** (**Ctrl+K** on Windows and Linux), or tap the search box in the top bar, and type the start of any word.

## Finding things

- Tasks, groups, cards and planned events all show in one list, grouped by kind, with the best matches first.
- Type the start of a word: “pla” finds “Plan the week”. Words can come in any order, and capitals don’t matter.
- Each result says where it lives: a task’s group and parent tasks, a card’s board and column, an event’s day and time.
- Card notes are searched too. When only the notes match, a short snippet shows the matching part.
- Done tasks and archived cards are found as well, after the open ones, and marked **Done** or **Archived**.
- A repeating event shows once, at its next time, marked **Repeats**.
- **Show all** lists every match of one kind.

## Opening and starting

- **↑** and **↓** move through the results, and **Enter** opens one: a task in its group, a card on its board, an event on its day in the calendar. What you opened is highlighted for a moment.
- An archived card opens in **Archived cards**, where you can restore it.
- **Start** on a task or card, or **⌘Enter**, starts tracking it straight away and closes search, leaving you on the page you were on. On an event linked to a task, **Start** starts that task.

## Privacy

- Searches run against your own data only, and what you type isn’t kept or logged.
