Cards now follow the work. Starting one can move it into your doing column, finishing its last task can move it to done, and cards left sitting in a column can archive themselves.

## Card triggers

- A card's details have a **Workflow** section with two pickers: **When started, move to** and **When finished, move to**. Each is a column on the card's board, or **Don’t move**.
- The started trigger fires every time you start the card. A card that's already in that column stays where it is.
- The finished trigger fires when the card's last open linked task is done. A card with no linked tasks never finishes, and reopening or linking a task doesn't fire it.
- If a trigger's column is deleted, the picker is flagged until you choose another.

## History

- A **History** section lists the card's moves, newest first, with where it came from, where it went and why: moved by hand, by a schedule, started, finished a task, archived or restored.
- **Show older moves** pages back through the rest.

## The card archive

- **Archive** takes a card off the board and pauses its schedules. An archived card's details show when and how it was archived, with **Restore**.
- The **Archived** button in the board header lists the board's archived cards, newest first, each with **Restore** and **Delete**.
- Restoring puts the card back at the top of the column it left and resumes only the schedules archiving paused.

## Idle cards

- **Board settings → Columns** has an **Archive after** length for each column, typed like `3d`, `2w` or `1m`, and combinable (`1m 2w`), up to a year. Leave it empty to keep cards forever.
- A column with a length shows it under its name, like "Archives after 1w".
- The clock counts from the card's last move, so editing a card doesn't reset it. A card with a running timer is never archived.
- Idle cards are archived within a minute or two of their time, marked **by idle**.
