DaiBX started as a nested to-do list. Version 3 rebuilds it from scratch as one workspace for **tasks, a board and a calendar**, so the same piece of work shows up in all three without being copied.

This release is the design: every screen of the app and this site, with nothing wired in yet. The Rust API lands next.

## What’s in it

- **Tasks** keep what made DaiBX useful: groups, and tasks nested as deep as the job needs. Create, edit and delete at any level.
- **Board** gives you lists, cards and checklists for the big picture.
- **Calendar** shows a week of what you planned and what you actually did.
- **Workflows** reset a checklist, move a card back to To do and block time on a schedule.

## Start a task, and the calendar records it

Starting a task opens a calendar event. Finishing it sets the end. If a task runs past its estimate, a check-in asks whether you’re still on it, and when you finish after a missed check-in, DaiBX suggests your last confirmed time as the end. You can always set the end time yourself.

## Under the hood

- The frontend moves from SolidJS to **Next.js**.
- The API will be **Rust and Axum** on AWS Lambda, with **Postgres** on Supabase as the one store.
