The board is live. It holds the bigger picture, while tasks keep the steps, and the two now point at each other.

## Boards, lists and cards

- Make as many **boards** as you need and switch between them from the board name. DaiBX opens the one you used last.
- Add, rename, reorder and delete **lists**. Deleting a list or a board tells you how many cards go with it first.
- Add **cards** straight into a list, then drag them within a list or across lists. On a phone or from the keyboard, use **Move to…** on the card and **Move left / Move right** on the list.
- Every change shows at once and rolls back with a message if the server refuses it.

## Properties

- Each board has its own **properties**, set in Board settings: **choice** (options with an optional color), **text** or **number**.
- A card's choice values show as colored chips on the board. Text and numbers stay inside the card.
- Rename, recolor and reorder as you go. Deleting a property or an option tells you how many cards use it and clears it from them.

## Inside a card

- A **due date** with an optional time. Cards past due are marked overdue.
- **Notes** in Markdown, starting from the outline Outcome, Context, Open questions and Done when. Links open in a new tab, and raw HTML shows as plain text.
- The **tasks** made from the card, with how many are done. Each one opens its group on the Tasks page.

## Planning with tasks

- **Copy for planning** in a list's menu copies its cards as Markdown, each with its card id, due date, properties and notes, ready for the daily plan.
- A group import can name a card on each top-level task. The task then shows its card on the Tasks page, and clicking it opens the board with that card open.
