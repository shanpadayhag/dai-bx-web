DaiBX now proposes when to do your tasks. Press **Plan** on the calendar and it fills your free time with the tasks that have an estimate, most urgent first. Nothing is saved until you accept.

## Plan your day or week

- **Plan** on the calendar plans **Today** or the **Next 7 days**, from every group or only the ones you tick. The choice is remembered on this device.
- Proposed blocks show on the calendar with a dashed outline. Each task gets one block in the earliest free quarter hour, never over an event already there.
- Tasks are placed by due date, then priority, then their place in the group. The same tasks and calendar always give the same plan.
- A block that ends after its task is due is marked **Late**.
- The **Plan** panel lists every block, and the tasks it couldn't place and why: no estimate, already planned, running now, doesn't fit, or too many.

## Review before it's saved

- Drag or resize a proposed block to move it. It stays where you put it.
- **Reject** a block and the plan is worked out again without that task, so later tasks move into the freed time.
- **Accept** one block or **Accept all** to turn them into planned events linked to their tasks. **Discard** drops the plan.

## What the planner reads

- Top-level tasks get **Planning…** in their menu: a priority (High, Normal or Low), a due date with an optional time, and an optional **plan between** window, like 22:00–02:00. Each shows as a chip on the task.
- A task with no due date uses its card's.
- **Settings → Working hours** gains **Plan in my working hours**. Turn it off to set each day's planning hours, or take a day off.
- A group that shows only on some days, or at some times, has its tasks planned only then.

## Planned events and tasks

- A planned event can link to a top-level task: pick its group, then the task.
- A linked event shows the task and its group with a **Start** button, which starts tracking the task.
- When the task is done, its event shows a check and a muted title.
- Repeating events and card schedule blocks don't link to tasks.
